Privacy Policy

App: Promptora AI

Last Updated: October 2026

1 Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Promptora AI. We value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how Promptora AI operates, what data is processed, and our strict local-first privacy commitments.

Key Summary: Promptora AI is designed with an offline-first architecture. We do not require registration or accounts, and we do not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personal identifying information (PII).

2 Zero Personal Data Collection

When you use Promptora AI, we do not require you to provide any personal data:

  • No sign-up, email, login, or social authentication required.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or personal files.
  • No biometric, health, financial, or payment information collected.
  • No continuous background tracking or GPS location access.

3 Local Device Storage

All user interactions within Promptora AI remain strictly on your device:

  • Favorites & Bookmarks: Saved prompts are stored locally in an encrypted on-device SQLite / Room database.
  • Recent Searches: Search queries are saved locally to assist with auto-suggestions and can be wiped instantly from the Settings screen.
  • Theme Preferences: Dark, Light, or System theme choices are saved locally in device shared preferences.

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage deletes this data permanently.

4 Advertising & Google AdMob

Promptora AI is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Advertisements help keep this prompt library completely free for all creators.

To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process certain device telemetry in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad attribution and frequency capping.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location inferred from IP address for regional ad relevance.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Crash logs and ad load latency to ensure stability.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:

Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps.

Your Privacy Choices: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads, or manage consent preferences directly in Promptora AI under Settings → Ad Privacy & Consent Choices.

5 Permissions Used

Promptora AI only requests permissions strictly required for its core functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google AdMob to load advertisements and to retrieve public preview image assets when online.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide subtle, tactile haptic feedback when copying prompts or toggling favorites. Can be disabled in app settings.

6 Clipboard & External AI Tools

When you tap "Copy Prompt", Promptora AI places the selected prompt text into your system clipboard for your convenience. Promptora AI does not monitor, transmit, or retain clipboard contents. You maintain complete control over where you paste your prompts (e.g. Nano Banana, Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion).

7 Children's Privacy

Promptora AI complies with COPPA and Google Play Developer Program policies. The application is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8 Security & Data Retention

Because Promptora AI does not operate external user database servers, your user data cannot be breached from our end. All local data is isolated within Android's sandboxed application container and managed by the Android operating system.

9 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal regulations. Any changes will be updated directly within this embedded policy file inside the app.
